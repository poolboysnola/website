Pool Service FAQs for New Orleans

Have questions about weekly pool cleaning, chemicals, scheduling, storm prep, saltwater pools, or green pool recovery? Here are answers to the questions we hear most from pool owners throughout Greater New Orleans.

Scheduling & Service

Questions About Pool Service

How do I know which day you'll come?

Pool Boys NOLA assigns your pool a regular weekly service day. We keep your service on a consistent schedule so you know when to expect us.

Do you charge more for the Northshore or the far Westbank?

Service pricing depends on location and the pool itself. Contact Pool Boys NOLA with your address and pool details so we can confirm whether your property is within our service area and provide pricing.

Can you service a pool I don't live at?

Yes. Pool Boys NOLA can service pools at rental properties, second homes, investment properties, and other properties where the owner does not live on site.

What happens during hurricane season?

During hurricane season, we can help pool owners prepare their pools before storms and address common pool problems after storms, including debris, rainwater, and changes in water chemistry.

Do you take commercial accounts outside these parishes?

Commercial and larger properties outside our normal service area may be considered depending on the property and service requirements. Contact Pool Boys NOLA to discuss your location and needs.

How fast can you get out for a first visit?

First-visit availability depends on our current schedule and your location. Contact Pool Boys NOLA and we will let you know the next available appointment.

Do I need to be home?

Usually, no. As long as we have access to the pool and the necessary equipment, you generally do not need to be home during routine pool service.

Pool Care Questions

Chemicals, Storms, Saltwater & Green Pools

Are chemicals included?

Yes. Our weekly pool maintenance service includes the chemicals needed for routine water care, including liquid chlorine, stabilizer, muriatic acid, sodium bicarbonate, and calcium when needed.

What happens after a big rain or a storm?

Heavy rain can dilute pool chemicals, change water chemistry, and bring debris into the pool. We inspect the pool, clean debris, test the water, and make the necessary chemical adjustments during service.

My pool is already green. Should I start here?

If your pool is already green, our Green Pool Recovery service is the better place to start. We address the existing water condition before moving into regular weekly maintenance.

Do you cover my street?

Pool Boys NOLA serves pool owners throughout our Greater New Orleans service area. If you are unsure whether your street is covered, contact us with your address and we can confirm availability.

Our Process

What Happens During Weekly Pool Service?

Every pool is different, but our weekly maintenance follows a straightforward routine designed to keep your pool clean and your water properly maintained.

1. Inspect We look over the pool, equipment, and water condition.
2. Clean We skim, brush, vacuum, and remove debris as needed.
3. Test We test the pool water to check its current chemistry.
4. Balance We add the appropriate chemicals when adjustments are needed.
5. Check We check the pool and equipment for anything that needs attention.
6. Service We leave the pool clean, balanced, and ready for you.
