Scheduling

Service-area questions, answered.

How do I know which day you'll come?

Your day is assigned by route when you sign up, and then it stays the same week to week. If a holiday or a storm moves it, we tell you in advance rather than just skipping.

Do you charge more for the Northshore or the far Westbank?

Pricing is based on your pool — size, system, and how often we visit — not your zip code. Outlying addresses are grouped onto set days to keep the drive time from turning into a surcharge.

Can you service a pool I don't live at?

Yes — rentals, second homes, and vacation properties are a big part of what we do. We can work from a lockbox or gate code and send the service notes straight to your phone.

What happens during hurricane season?

Ahead of a named storm we'll secure loose equipment, adjust water level, and shock the pool so it's in the best shape to ride it out. Afterward, storm cleanups are prioritized by damage, not by route order.

Do you take commercial accounts outside these parishes?

Sometimes. For HOAs, hotels, and apartment complexes we'll travel further than we would for a single residential pool. Send the details and we'll give you a straight yes or no.

How fast can you get out for a first visit?

In the denser parts of the metro, usually the same week. On the edges of the map it depends on which day that route runs — we'll tell you the actual date, not a vague window.
