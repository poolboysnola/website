Our story

It started with somebody else's green pool.

Every pool person has the story: a neighbor's pool goes from blue to swamp over one warm, rainy week, three companies quote three completely different fixes, and nobody explains why. We got tired of watching that happen.

So we built the version we'd want for our own backyard — a crew that measures before it recommends, that services salt cells and chlorine feeders with equal enthusiasm, and that leaves a note after every visit so you know exactly what your water is doing.

Why New Orleans pools are their own thing

Heat, humidity, and a genuinely long swim season mean our pools work harder than most. Then add the specifics: sudden afternoon downpours that dilute your sanitizer, live oak debris that clogs baskets and stains plaster, spring pollen, and a hurricane season that needs an actual plan rather than a shrug.

A route built for a dry climate doesn't survive here. Ours is built for this one — tighter chemical checks after heavy rain, filter attention when the oaks drop, and equipment protection before a storm rather than after.

What we're not

We're not a franchise with a call center three states away, and we're not going to sell you a heater you don't need. If the honest answer is "your pool is fine, see you next week," that's the answer you get.

How we work

Six promises, no fine print.

These aren't values on a wall — they're the things we'd want from a pool company if we were the ones paying the invoice.

Test, then talk

Every recommendation starts with a reading. If we can't show you the number behind it, we don't charge you for it.

Plain English only

Cyanuric acid, total alkalinity, saturation index — we'll happily explain all of it, but never hide behind it.

Same day, same window

Your pool gets a fixed day each week. If we're running late, you hear it from us before you notice.

Chemicals included

Routine sanitizer and balancers are part of the plan, not a surprise line item at the end of a hot month.

Fluent in both systems

Salt cell or tablet feeder, we maintain each one the way it's meant to be maintained — no converting you for convenience.

Local, and staying that way

One metro, one crew, no call center. The person who answers the phone knows your pool.
