The science bit

Two systems. One set of geeks fluent in both.

Most pool guys pick a lane. We didn't. Here's the honest rundown on what each system actually needs — no upsell, just chemistry.

Salt water

Low-drama, still a system

  • A salt cell quietly turns dissolved salt into chlorine, so you're not dosing tablets by hand.
  • Still a chlorine pool underneath — the generator just makes its own supply.
  • pH tends to climb faster and needs closer watching than a standard setup.
  • Salt is gentle on skin, less gentle on ladders and fittings — we use salt-rated gear.
VS
Chlorine

Familiar, hands-on, effective

  • Tablets or liquid added on a schedule — simple to understand, easy to source.
  • Generally the stronger option against algae, which matters in shaded or high-use pools.
  • Needs more frequent manual balancing, especially after rain or heavy swim days.
  • Lower equipment cost upfront, with simpler repairs down the line.
